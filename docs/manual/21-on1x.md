\page ccweave-manual-21 Chapter 21: On1x profile

On1x is the dynamic-execution profile. It adds first-class dynamic dispatch
sites, inline-cache slots, deoptimization metadata, and safepoint
annotations.

These constructs are the concrete divergence from Tilly. The rest of the
type, node, construction, text, and Glue machinery remains shared.

Hosts may expose profile-specific Glue accessors for these facilities.
Portable kernels must feature-test extensions with `glue-has?` before using
them.
